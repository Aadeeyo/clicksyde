Privacy and cookies

Your details, handled with care.

What this website collects, why, and how to ask us to change or delete it. Last updated 27 November 2026.

Who we are

Clicksyde is a PR, digital marketing and talent management agency in Lagos, Nigeria. We decide how the personal data collected on this website is used, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Questions go to .

What we collect and why

  • The “Let's talk” form. Your name, business, what you need, budget, timeline, how to reach you and anything you add. We use it to reply to you and prepare for a first conversation. The basis is your request to hear from us.
  • The newsletter (The Syde Note). Your email address, to send you the newsletter. The basis is your consent; every issue lets you unsubscribe.
  • Analytics, only if you accept cookies. Google Analytics records which pages are visited, roughly where visitors come from, the device used, and actions such as sending the form or tapping our email or WhatsApp links. IP addresses are shortened. We use it to see what helps people and improve the site. If you decline, it never loads.

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising profiles.

Cookies and browser storage

  • Your cookie choice is kept in your browser so we don't ask on every page.
  • Google Analytics cookies (names starting _ga) are set only after you accept, and last up to two years.

You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Who else handles it

Form answers and newsletter sign-ups are stored with Supabase, our database provider. Analytics data is processed by Google. These providers may store data outside Nigeria, under their own security and data-transfer safeguards. Only the Clicksyde team can read form answers.

How long we keep it

Form answers: up to two years after our last conversation, unless you become a client. Newsletter: until you unsubscribe. Analytics: 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw your consent. Email and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
